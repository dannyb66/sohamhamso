## Rules applied

- **Meaning:** your English (translation and every English gloss) is the confirmed meaning. The Hindi does not add, drop or change any claim, agent, relation, number, name, negation or sentence type.
- **Vocabulary:** Sanskrit-leaning literary Hindi (tatsama words). Where the English was ambiguous, the reading that agrees with the Sanskrit was used.
- **Where your English seems to differ from the Sanskrit,** the Hindi still follows your English. The point is recorded in the notes below for you to decide.
- **Consistent terms:** "non-dual" → अद्वैत (अद्वय where the verse has advaya); "non-conceptual" → निर्विकल्प; "moment" → क्षण (never मुहूर्त); "O sage" follows the Sanskrit vocative (ब्रह्मन् → हे ब्रह्मन्, मुने → हे मुने).
- **Verses wrapped in quotation marks** in your English (most of Ch 13–15) are wrapped in “ ” in the Hindi too; quotes inside them use ‘ ’.
- **Sanskrit lines** are unchanged byte for byte. The word-by-word Sanskrit terms are converted from your IAST by script, so they carry your IAST spellings (including the typos listed below). prasahēt and bhāsayēt (Ch 14) were set with plain "e".
