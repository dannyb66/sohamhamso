## Layout (matches the printed Part 1, not the drifted 6x9 docx)

- 6×9 in. Top and bottom margins 0.76". Mirrored margins: inside 0.76" + 0.14" gutter, outside 0.6". Header and footer 0.35" from the edge.
- Front matter: title, copyright (2026), dedication (printed wording), blank, Contents, blank. No page numbers or running heads.
- Every chapter starts on a right-hand page, numbered 1 from Ch 12 (Part 2) and Ch 18 (Part 3).
- Running heads: TRIPURA RAHASYA on left pages, SOHAM HAMSO on right pages. Page number centered at the bottom. Chapter opening pages have neither.
- Fonts: Calibri, with Mangal for Devanagari. Verse block styled as in Part 1; one blank line between verses.
- Verses are never split across pages (each verse is kept whole on one page). The last verse of each chapter stays on the same page as the chapter's closing lines, so a chapter's final page may hold just one verse plus those lines.
- One deliberate difference: the Contents dot leaders run the full text width, so the long Ch 19 and Ch 20 titles fit on one line.
