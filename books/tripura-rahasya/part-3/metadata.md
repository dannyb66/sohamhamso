## Known issue: stale table of contents in the PDF

The Word-exported PDF (345 pages) has a stale table of contents: page numbers are off by 2 from **Chapter 19** onward. Before uploading, open the DOCX in Word, right-click the TOC and choose **Update Field** (update entire table), then export the PDF again.
