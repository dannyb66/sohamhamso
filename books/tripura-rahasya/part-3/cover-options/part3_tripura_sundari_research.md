## Iconography checklist
- Four arms: noose (pasha), goad (ankusha), sugarcane bow, five flower arrows
- Rose-red complexion, crescent moon in the crown, often three eyes, a youthful sixteen-year-old (Shodashi) form
- The five-Brahma throne: Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra and Ishvara as the legs, Sadashiva as the seat (Ch 20 v37–38)
- Lakshmi and Saraswati with chamaras (fly-whisks); a parasol; the Sri Chakra
- Abode from the text: Manidvipa (island of jewels) in an ocean of nectar, kadamba forest, wish-jewel (chintamani) mansion (Ch 20 v36–37)
- Dattatreya (version B): three heads, exactly six arms (conch, discus, trident, damaru, water pot, rosary), four dogs, white cow; Parashurama with his axe

## Verses from Part 3 (verbatim from the manuscript)
**20.37** (main picture source)
मणिद्वीपे नीपवने चिन्तामणिसुमन्दिरे । / पञ्चब्रह्ममये मञ्चे रूपं त्रैपुरसुन्दरम्‌ ॥ ३७ ॥
maṇidvīpe nīpavane cintāmaṇi-sumandire । / pañcabrahmamaye mañce rūpaṁ traipurasundaram ॥ 37 ॥
In the island of jewels, in the kadamba forest, in the gem mansion, on the throne composed of five Brahmas, is the form of Tripurasundari. *(A1, A2)*

**20.36**
परा सा प्रतिभा देव्याः परं रूपं ममेरितम्‌ । / ब्रह्माण्डानामनेकानां बहिरूर्ध्वे सुधाम्बुधौ ॥ ३६ ॥
parā sā pratibhā devyāḥ paraṁ rūpaṁ mameritam । / brahmāṇḍānām anekānāṁ bahir ūrdhve sudhāmbudhau ॥ 36 ॥
The supreme brilliance of the goddess, the supreme form, spoken by me, beyond and above many universes, in the ocean of nectar. *(A3)*

**20.22**
अथ प्रोचुरृषिगणा विद्यां तां त्रिपुरेश्वरीम्‌ । / नमस्तुभ्यं महेशानि श्रीविद्ये त्रिपुरेश्वरि ॥ २२ ॥
atha procur ṛṣigaṇā vidyāṁ tāṁ tripureśvarīm । / namas tubhyaṁ maheśāni śrīvidye tripureśvari ॥ 22 ॥
Then the group of sages spoke that knowledge of Tripureshwari. Salutation to you, O great Goddess, O sacred knowledge, O ruler of Tripura. *(B1)*

**19.51**
परा सा या चितिर्देवी स्वस्वातन्त्र्यस्य वैभवात्‌ । / स्वात्मन्येव जगच्चित्रं दर्पणप्रतिबिम्बवत्‌ ॥ ५१ ॥
parā sā yā citiḥ devī sva-svātantryasya vaibhavāt । / svātmani eva jagat-citraṁ darpaṇa-pratibimbavat ॥ 51 ॥
The supreme consciousness, which is the goddess, by the power of her own freedom, reflects the picture of the world in herself, like a reflection in a mirror. *(B3: the mirror motif, which also echoes Part 1 v1)*

**22.105** (recommended back-cover quote)
अखण्डाद्वयचिच्छक्तिस्त्रिपुरैवावभासिनी । / सैवाविद्या च विद्या च बन्धो मोक्षश्च साधनम्‌ ॥ १०५ ॥
akhaṇḍa-advaya-cit-śaktiḥ tripurā eva avabhāsinī । / sā eva avidyā ca vidyā ca bandhaḥ mokṣaḥ ca sādhanam ॥ 105 ॥
The unbroken non-dual consciousness power, Tripura, indeed, shining, she indeed is ignorance and knowledge, bondage and liberation, and the means.

**22.112**
सर्वात्मभूतं यद्रूपं विचार्यावगतं स्फुटम्‌ । / मुक्तिः स्यादन्यथा बन्धः सा भवेत्‌ त्रिपुरैव ह्रीम्‌ ॥ ११२ ॥
sarvātma-bhūtaṁ yad-rūpaṁ vicāryā avagataṁ sphuṭam । / muktiḥ syāt anyathā bandhaḥ sā bhavet tripura eva hrīm ॥ 112 ॥
Whose form is all-soul-prevading, clearly understood by consideration, liberation is attained. Otherwise, it may be bondage. She indeed is Tripura, hrim. *(B2: the teaching that leads to the vision)*

## Prompt pack (Gemini; 2:3 portrait)
### Version A: Tripura Sundari only

**A1**
> Painting in the style of early-1900s Ravi Varma Press oleographs, soft realism. Goddess Tripura Sundari (Lalita), rose-red complexion, crescent moon in her crown, serene smile, exactly four arms holding a noose, a goad, a sugarcane bow and five flower arrows. She sits on a throne whose four legs are Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra and Ishvara, with Sadashiva as the seat. Setting: Manidvipa, the island of jewels in an ocean of nectar, a kadamba grove and a gem-studded pavilion behind her. Lakshmi and Saraswati fan her with chamaras. Warm crimson, gold and soft pearl light, central focus on the Goddess. Portrait 2:3, calmer lower third for title text. No text, no lettering, no border or painted frame, no watermark.

**A2**
> South Indian Mysore-school painting with raised gesso gilding and delicate jewel colours. Frontal, iconic Goddess Rajarajeshwari (Tripura Sundari), exactly four arms holding a noose, a goad, a sugarcane bow and five flower arrows, seated on a lotus over the reclining Sadashiva on a throne upheld by Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra and Ishvara. Lakshmi and Saraswati with chamaras at her sides, a parasol above. Background: carved temple pillars and a glimpse of a kadamba forest, not a painted frame. Palette: ruby red, emerald, ivory and gold. Portrait 2:3, calmer lower third for title text. No text, no lettering, no border or painted frame, no watermark.

**A3**
> Mystical devotional painting. Goddess Tripura Sundari, rose-gold skin, exactly four arms holding a noose, a goad, a sugarcane bow and five flower arrows, seated on a pink lotus at the glowing bindu centre of a vast luminous Sri Chakra floating above an ocean of nectar. Around and below, countless tiny universes drift like glowing orbs, showing her beyond and above many universes. Soft radiance, fine geometric light lines. Palette: deep indigo night, rose, saffron and gold. Portrait 2:3, calmer lower third for title text. No text, no lettering, no border or painted frame, no watermark.

### Version B: Tripura Sundari with Dattatreya

**B1**
> Indian miniature painting, Pahari / Kangra style, gouache with gold. Above, in a radiant golden aureole among clouds, Goddess Tripura Sundari appears, exactly four arms holding a noose, a goad, a sugarcane bow and five flower arrows, seated on the five-Brahma throne. Below, in a kadamba grove, Lord Dattatreya (three heads, exactly six arms holding conch, discus, trident, damaru, water pot and rosary) stands with the young sage Parashurama and a few sages, hands folded in praise, looking up at her. Four dogs and a white cow nearby. Soft greens, rose and gold. Portrait 2:3, calmer lower third for title text. No text, no lettering, no border or painted frame, no watermark.

**B2**
> Painting in the style of early-1900s Ravi Varma Press oleographs. Foreground: Lord Dattatreya, three heads and exactly six arms holding conch, discus, trident, damaru, water pot and rosary, seated on a deerskin on a riverbank, teaching the young sage Parashurama, who kneels with his axe beside him; four dogs and a white cow nearby. Behind and above them, as a luminous vision in the evening sky, Goddess Tripura Sundari enthroned, exactly four arms holding a noose, a goad, a sugarcane bow and five flower arrows. Sunset gold, crimson and violet. Portrait 2:3, calmer lower third for title text. No text, no lettering, no border or painted frame, no watermark.

**B3**

Four dogs and a white cow at his sides. Palette: vermilion, deep blue and gold. Portrait 2:3, calmer lower third for title text. No text, no lettering, no border or painted frame, no watermark.
