Subject: Permission request — use of Muktabodha e-texts in an open scholarly Sanskrit corpus

Dear Muktabodha Indological Research Institute,

I am writing to request permission regarding e-texts from the Muktabodha
Digital Library, on behalf of **sohamhamso** (https://sohamhamso.org), a
non-commercial scholarly project building an openly accessible, verse-aligned
corpus of Tantric and Kashmir Śaiva scriptures.

**About the project.** sohamhamso presents Sanskrit texts verse by verse
(Devanāgarī, IAST, and other Indic scripts) alongside word-level glosses and
translations into English and twelve Indian languages. Translations are
AI-assisted, grounded in the Sanskrit with public-domain renderings as
reference signals, and carry full provenance metadata (model, prompt version,
review status). The project also publishes periodic dataset releases (CSV,
JSON, TEI) under CC-BY-SA 4.0, archived with DOIs via Zenodo, for use by
researchers and students. Source attribution is preserved per text and per
release; Muktabodha would be credited prominently as the source of any text
derived from your library.

**Texts concerned.** We would like to use the following Muktabodha e-texts:

- *Pratyabhijñāhṛdayam* (Kṣemarāja) — currently prepared, publication held pending your permission
- *Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra* — currently prepared, publication held pending your permission

and, as the corpus grows:

- *Gītārthasaṃgraha* (Abhinavagupta; Muktabodha M00244) — prepared, held pending permission
- *Mahānirvāṇa Tantra* ullāsas 1–3 (Muktabodha M00049) — prepared, held pending permission
- *Śivadṛṣṭi* (Somānanda; Muktabodha M00081) — prospective, sourcing deferred
- *Tantrasāra* (Abhinavagupta) — prepared (note: our copy is the public-domain GRETIL/KSTS-1918 e-text, so it may not require this permission; included for completeness)

**Our two requests.** Because these are distinct uses, we would be grateful
for your answer to each separately:

1. **Public web display.** May we display the Sanskrit text of the above
   works on https://sohamhamso.org as a free, browse-only reader, with
   attribution to Muktabodha on every page on which the text appears?

2. **Dataset redistribution and derivative translations.** May we (a) include
   the Sanskrit text of the above works in our openly licensed dataset
   releases (CC-BY-SA 4.0, deposited on Zenodo), and (b) publish and
   redistribute AI-derived translations and word-level glosses of these works
   under the same license? If you prefer a different license or specific
   attribution wording for redistributed copies, we will gladly adopt it.

If permission is granted for only one of these uses, we will honor that
boundary exactly — our release tooling already enforces a per-text hold on
dataset publication. We are equally happy to discuss conditions, share our
methodology in more detail, or provide a preview of how your texts would be
presented and credited.

Thank you for the extraordinary resource the Digital Library represents, and
for considering this request.
